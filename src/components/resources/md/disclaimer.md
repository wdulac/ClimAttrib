WeatherAttrib is a research web application developed through collaboration between CNRM and LSCE, notably via the PEPR TRACCS program. The figures and results provided are the result of calculations based on global climate simulations from the CMIP6 database and on data from the ERA5 reanalysis, considered here as observations. The outputs generated by the application do not imply any responsibility on the part of the project's partner laboratories and institutes (CEA, CNRS, IPSL, Météo-France, UVSQ).
