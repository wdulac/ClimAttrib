WeatherAttrib is a research driven web application developed through collaboration between CNRM and LSCE, notably via the PEPR TRACCS program. The figures and results provided are the result of calculations based on global climate simulations from the CMIP6 database (Eyring et al. 2016) and on data from the ERA5 reanalysis (Hersbach et al. 2020), considered here as observations. The figures and results provided by the application do not imply any responsibility on the part of the project's partner laboratories and institutes (CEA, CNRS, IPSL, Météo-France, UVSQ).
